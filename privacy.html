Privacy Policy

Future Funded · last updated 7 August 2026

Future Funded is a private, on-device planning calculator. It does not collect, transmit, store on any server, or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We do not have a server that receives your data.

Your inputs

The figures you enter (ages, balances, contributions, return and inflation assumptions, etc.) are saved only in your own browser or device storage so the app can remember them between visits. They stay on your device, are never sent to us or anyone else, and you can clear them at any time by using your browser/app storage controls or the app's Reset button.

Network use

The installed app runs fully offline; it does not call out to any service with your data. (The website version loads its charting library from a public CDN to display the page — this involves no personal data.)

Children

The app is a general-purpose financial planning tool and is not directed at children.

Not financial advice

The app is a planning model for illustration only and does not constitute financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
