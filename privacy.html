Privacy Policy

Future Funded · last updated 22 August 2026

Future Funded is a planning calculator that runs in your browser. The figures you type stay on your own device unless you choose to create an account. Separately, we record a small amount of anonymous usage data — which parts of the tool get used, and where people give up — so we can make it better. That is described in full below.

The two ways you can use it

  • Signed out (the default). Every figure you type stays in your browser's local storage. None of your numbers are sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Signed in (optional). If you create an account so your plan follows you between devices, your figures are stored on our behalf by Supabase, our hosting provider.

What is stored if you create an account

  • Your email address, and a display name if you choose to set one.
  • Your plan — the figures you enter: ages, pot balances, contributions, target income, return and inflation assumptions, and your display preferences. These are held as a single record tied to your account.

We never ask for and never receive your name, address, bank details, account numbers, or any connection to a real financial provider. Nothing you enter is verified against anything — the app has no idea whether your numbers are real, and it does not need to.

Usage data

To know whether the tool is any use, we record a short log of what happened, never what you typed. Each entry contains:

  • A random device identifier stored in your browser. It is generated on this site, means nothing anywhere else, and is not linked to your name, email or IP address unless you are signed in.
  • A session identifier, which changes after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  • The name of the thing that happened — for example "opened advanced assumptions", "changed target income", "completed a plan". The full list is fixed in advance and enforced by the database.
  • Whether you are on a phone, tablet or desktop. Not your exact device, browser or screen size.
  • Your account id, only if you are signed in.
  • Your browser's timezone — the setting itself, such as "Europe/London" — sent once when a visit starts, so we can see roughly which countries the tool is reaching. This is not a location lookup: your IP address is not examined and no geolocation service is contacted. If you use a VPN or you are abroad, it simply reads as that country instead.

Your balances, contributions, payments and target income are never sent as usage data. Two things the tool works out are recorded, so we can see what it is telling people:

  • The optionality age it calculated for you, so we can report a typical figure across everyone.
  • If you use the “Mortgage or invest?” calculator: which way it came out (overpay or invest), the comparison period you picked, and — as a band rather than a figure, such as “4–6%” — roughly what investment return would have been needed to draw level. It is banded deliberately, because the exact number would sit close to your own mortgage rate. Your mortgage balance, payment and spare cash are not recorded.

We do not record the pages you came from, the pages you go to, your IP address, or anything that would let us follow you to another website.

Turning it off. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is recorded at all — no identifier is created and no entries are written. Clearing your browser storage also resets the identifier, at which point you look like a brand-new visitor.

Aggregate by default. The internal dashboard we look at reports totals, medians and conversion rates. Its usage figures are aggregated inside the database and only summary numbers leave it, and medians are withheld entirely until enough people have saved a plan for one to be meaningless about any individual.

When an administrator can see your figures. There is one exception to the above, and we would rather tell you about it than leave it out. If you have an account, an administrator can open your saved plan — the ages, balances, contributions and targets you entered — in order to answer a support question or work out why the tool is doing something wrong. It is read-only: nothing they do can change what you have saved. Every such view is written to a permanent log recording who looked, whose plan they opened, when, and why, and that log cannot be edited or deleted from within the app. If you would rather this were never possible, use the tool signed out — signed out, your figures are only ever in your own browser and nobody can look at them at all.

Our lawful basis for this is legitimate interests — understanding and improving a tool we offer. You can object to it at any time by asking whoever shared this tool with you.

Feedback you send us

There's a suggestion box in the top bar. If you use it, we store what you wrote, when you wrote it, and — so that a problem can actually be reproduced — your browser and operating system, your screen size, your timezone, your language, and whether you're running it as an installed app. If you're signed in, your account is attached; if you're not, nothing identifies you beyond what you choose to put in the message.

The email address field is optional and used for one thing: replying to you. Leave it blank and we have no way to contact you, which is fine.

None of your plan is included — no balances, no contributions, no target income. Please don't type figures you'd rather we didn't see into the message itself. Feedback is kept until it's dealt with and then deleted; ask and we'll delete yours sooner.

What we still don't do

No advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking services, no cookies used for tracking, no profiling, and no selling or sharing of your data with anyone. The usage data above goes to our own database and nowhere else. We don't email you except for the sign-in, email-confirmation and password-reset messages you trigger yourself.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase — authentication, storage of your account, your saved plan and the usage data above, and delivery of sign-in and password-reset emails.
  • GitHub Pages — serves this website. Like any web host, its servers see the usual request information (including your IP address) needed to deliver a page.
  • cdnjs and jsDelivr — public content networks that serve the charting and sign-in libraries the page needs. They receive the request for those files; no plan data is involved.

How long it's kept

Usage data is kept for up to 400 days and then deleted. Your plan, signed out: for as long as you leave it in your browser — clear it with your browser's storage controls or the app's own reset. Signed in: until it's deleted. Account deletion isn't yet self-service, so ask whoever shared this tool with you and your account and saved plan will be removed.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask for a copy of what's held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Since the only personal data involved is your email address and the figures you typed in yourself, these requests are straightforward to honour — ask whoever shared this tool with you.

Security

Your saved plan is protected by row-level security so that only your own signed-in account can read or write it. That said, this is a personal project, not a bank: please don't treat it as a vault for anything you would be uncomfortable seeing exposed, and use a password you don't reuse elsewhere.

Children

The app is a general-purpose financial planning tool and is not directed at children.

Not financial advice

The app is a planning model for illustration only and does not constitute financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.

Changes

About this policy

Future Funded is a personal project shared privately, not a commercial service. If you'd rather not have your figures stored at all, simply don't create an account — the app works fully signed out. If you'd rather not be counted in the usage data either, turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser, or ask and we'll remove your entries.
